Which files do I need?

All of them sit in the Diablo II install folder after installing Diablo II + Lord of Destruction (patch 1.14d).

Required to play: d2data.mpq, d2exp.mpq, d2char.mpq, patch_d2.mpq.

Recommended: d2sfx.mpq, d2speech.mpq — sound effects and voices.

Optional: d2music.mpq, d2xmusic.mpq, d2xtalk.mpq, d2video.mpq, d2xvideo.mpq — music, Act V voices and cinematics.

I do not encourage getting the files from illegal places. You can still buy the game on Battle.net (remember you need Lord of Destruction as well): Diablo II and Diablo II: Lord of Destruction. Or search for deals on sites like gg.deals.
